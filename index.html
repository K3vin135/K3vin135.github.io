Hermes Agent

A personal AI assistant I run on my own private server. It is the application behind the Google OAuth consent screen that may have brought you here.

Hermes Agent is single user software. There is one instance, it runs on a private server I control, and the only person who uses it is me. There is no public service, no sign up, and no accounts for anyone else.

I ask it to handle the parts of my day that are repetitive: reading through my inbox, drafting and sending replies, pulling details out of a thread so I can act on it, and keeping track of what still needs an answer. To do that on my behalf, it needs access to my own Gmail account through the Gmail API. That is the reason for the OAuth request.

Nothing it reads leaves my server or goes to a third party. Email content is handled in memory for the task at hand and is not collected into a database, it is not used to train models, and it is never sold or shared. The full detail, including Google's Limited Use requirements and how to revoke access, is in the privacy policy.
