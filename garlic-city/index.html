On Monterey Road since 2007.

Garlic City Cafe opened in 2007 in the middle of downtown Gilroy, a town that calls itself the Garlic Capital of the World and means it. We took the name seriously: garlic is in the soup, on the burger, folded into the bread, and yes, in the vanilla ice cream.

Diego runs the kitchen. Food is cooked when you order it, not pulled from a freezer, and the portions are built for people who worked that morning. Margarita and the rest of the floor know the regulars by what they order.

Inside, it is a small cafe with about a dozen tables, not a production line. Outside, the patio is pet friendly and the dog gets water without being asked. Street parking is right there on Monterey.
